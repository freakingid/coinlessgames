\## Somebody has to clean up low Earth orbit. It's never the people who made the mess.

It's 2050, and low Earth orbit is a junkyard. Billionaires launched satellites by the thousands to sell internet from the sky. Militaries filled it with spy satellites, then blew some of their own apart just to prove they could. When the hardware died, nobody came back for it. That part isn't science fiction: even today, radar tracks tens of thousands of objects up there, and most of them are dead.

You're Dan, a self-taught freelance salvage pilot with no flight school, no company behind you, and a ship you still owe the bank for. You're also the best pilot up there.

\## Blast. Hook. Haul.

\- \*\*Blast\*\* dead satellites into scrap.

\- \*\*Hook\*\* the scrap onto your tow chain, one piece at a time. The chain is your cargo, and it whips behind you as you fly.

\- \*\*Haul\*\* it to the recycling dock. Longer chains pay much more, but they're harder to fly and harder to protect.

![GIF: shooting a satellite apart, hooking the pieces, delivering the chain to a dock\](TODO-gif-blast-hook-haul.gif)

\## The junk fights back

Leave scrap floating too long and the old satellites' AI rebuilds it into Hunters that launch drones to ram you. Every Hunter is built from junk you didn't haul away. UFOs want you dead too, no explanation given.

![GIF: loose scrap glowing red and snapping together into a Hunter that launches drones\](TODO-gif-hunter-forms.gif)

\## Cash in, power up

Drag your haul into the recycling dock to get paid. Every delivery also earns upgrade tokens, and what you get is mostly the luck of the draw. Fly with what the dock deals you.

![GIF: a long chain delivered to the dock, upgrade tokens popping out\](TODO-gif-dock-delivery.gif)

\## One ship. Still financed.

Four shield pips, no extra lives. Your score is your paycheck in dollars, and every shift pays down the loan on your ship, the Atomic Dustbin. Pay it off for good, then keep flying for the business fund and the leaderboards.

\## Classic arcade, honest price

\- Rotate, thrust, fire: inertia-based flight in a wraparound orbit

\- Online leaderboards

\- Steam Cloud saves for your loan progress

\- One fair price. No microtransactions, nothing to unlock with real money.

\*The junk is real.\* At the end of 2024, the European Space Agency counted about 40,000 tracked objects in orbit, and only about 11,000 of them were working satellites ([ESA Space Environment Report 2025\](https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/Space_Debris/ESA_Space_Environment_Report_2025)). China in 2007 and Russia in 2021 each blew up one of their own satellites with a missile, leaving debris clouds that still threaten spacecraft today ([Ploughshares\](https://ploughshares.ca/reports/what-kinetic-asat-testing-tells-us-about-space-security-governance)).
